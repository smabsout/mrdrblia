name: Price Guide valuation engine
description: Location and invariants of the comp-based valuation engine in the Price Guide app
---

## Core invariant
Prices are NEVER generated by LLM. `sale_price`, `low_estimate`, `median_estimate`, and `high_estimate` come only from the weighted-percentile formulas in `artifacts/api-server/src/lib/valuation.ts`.

## Tier logic
- Tier A: ≥2 verified direct sales for the exact item → use those sales
- Tier B: <2 direct sales but comp_matches with similarity_score ≥ 0.75 → use verified sales from comps (top 10 by score)
- Tier C: no data → show category-wide range only, no numeric estimate

## Cache pattern
Results written to `valuation_cache` table. Invalidated on: sale insert, sale verify. Tier B items recomputed nightly (2 AM) via node-cron in `artifacts/api-server/src/lib/cron.ts`.

## Half-life constant
HALF_LIFE_DAYS = 180 (6 months). Exported as a named constant, not buried inline.

## How to apply
When adding new sale or verification endpoints, always call `computeValuation(itemId)` afterwards (fire-and-forget with .catch logging). Do not inline valuation logic in route handlers.
